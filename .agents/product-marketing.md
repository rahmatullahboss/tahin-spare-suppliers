# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Tahin Spare Suppliers is a Bangladesh-based marine engine, generator, hydraulic equipment, and ship spare-parts stockist/exporter serving vessel operators and ship-management buyers worldwide.
**What it does:** Tahin Spare Suppliers sources, inspects, reconditions, stocks, and exports marine propulsion engines, auxiliary engines, generator sets, deck-crane/hydraulic equipment, turbochargers, pumps, gearboxes, and engine spare parts. The website's primary commercial job is to make real inventory discoverable by brand/model/part number and convert search visitors into qualified quote enquiries.
**Product category:** Marine machinery supplier; marine engine supplier; ship spare-parts supplier/exporter.
**Product type:** B2B industrial supplier/exporter with an inventory-led website and CMS.
**Business model:** Quote-based B2B sales. Pricing is normally provided by enquiry rather than public checkout.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Ship owners, ship-management companies, vessel operators, fleet operators, shipyards, repair workshops, marine procurement companies, and technical-management firms worldwide.
**Decision-makers:** Technical Superintendents, Fleet Managers, Procurement/Purchasing Managers, Marine Engineers, Chief Engineers, Technical Managers, and ship-management buyers.
**Primary use case:** Find a specific marine engine, generator, component, model, or part quickly and request verified availability, specifications, price, and worldwide shipping.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Find an exact brand/model/part number from searchable inventory.
- Source used/reconditioned marine machinery with enough technical detail to evaluate fit.
- Obtain a fast quote and shipping/export support from Bangladesh.
**Use cases:**
- Replacement engine or genset sourcing after failure/overhaul planning.
- Spare-parts sourcing by OEM brand, model, and part number.
- Deck-crane/hydraulic component sourcing.
- Emergency vessel-downtime procurement.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Marine buyers often search with exact brand/model/part-number intent but supplier sites expose only broad categories, image-only listings, thin product pages, or outdated stock.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Generic category pages without model-level indexable pages.
- Product identifiers embedded only in photos.
- Weak stock/condition/location information.
- Slow quote paths and poor mobile experience.
- Duplicate/thin inventory pages that are difficult for search engines to understand.
**What it costs them:** Vessel downtime, procurement delays, wrong-part risk, repeated supplier outreach, and lost operating time.
**Emotional tension:** Buyers need confidence that the supplier has a real, compatible item and can respond quickly.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Other Chattogram/Bangladesh marine machinery and ship-spare exporters competing for model-level Google searches.
**Secondary:** International used-marine-equipment marketplaces and dealer networks.
**Indirect:** Offline brokers, WhatsApp-only sourcing, and existing supplier relationships.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Proximity to Chattogram ship-recycling/sourcing ecosystem.
- Inventory-led pages for exact models and parts.
- Reconditioned/used marine machinery expertise.
- Worldwide export/logistics support.
- Direct RFQ and WhatsApp conversion paths.
**How we do it differently:** Build indexable, content-rich pages around actual inventory entities rather than only generic marketing pages.
**Why that's better:** Buyers can validate relevance before contacting sales, while search engines can match exact commercial-intent queries.
**Why customers choose us:** Relevant stock, marine-specialist context, export capability, and fast contact options.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| Is the item actually available? | Every product should carry an explicit availability field and current photos; enquiry confirms final stock state. |
| Is the condition suitable? | Condition, technical specifications, application, and inspection/reconditioning notes are shown in HTML. |
| Can you ship internationally? | Product/category pages consistently expose worldwide export and quote paths. |

**Anti-persona:** Consumer buyers seeking automotive/land-engine retail parts or fixed-price ecommerce checkout for unrelated products.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Slow sourcing, thin supplier listings, unverified inventory, poor model searchability.
**Pull:** Exact model/part pages, real photos, clear condition/availability, quick quote/WhatsApp.
**Habit:** Existing broker/supplier network and manual WhatsApp sourcing.
**Anxiety:** Authenticity, compatibility, condition, shipment reliability, and response time.
